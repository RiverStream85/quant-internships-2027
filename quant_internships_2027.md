## How to use this report

Start with the open-company summary, then use the detailed applications table for role-level dates and links. The exhaustive directory at the end covers every firm in TraderMath’s directory, including firms for which TraderMath has too little interview evidence or no verified 2027 posting.

Interview lean is an evidence-based reading of TraderMath’s visible firm-tagged questions and firm-linked coding practice:

- **Math / reasoning-heavy:** tagged math, probability, reasoning, market-making or related questions are visible, but no firm-linked coding module was found.

- **Coding-heavy (visible evidence):** a firm-linked coding module is visible, but no firm-tagged math/reasoning questions were found.

- **Mixed / role-dependent:** both question-based and coding evidence are visible; the actual balance will depend heavily on whether the role is trading, research, or engineering.

- **Insufficient TraderMath evidence:** do not infer an easy or nontechnical process—only that TraderMath does not expose enough firm-specific material to classify it reliably.

Classification distribution: Insufficient TraderMath evidence 214; Math / reasoning-heavy 36; Mixed / role-dependent 20; Coding-heavy (visible evidence) 3.

## Methodology and caveats

- **Scope:** “Next year” is interpreted as calendar/recruiting year 2027 because this report was checked on 2026-08-24.

- **Opening verification:** the expanded audit combines current official ATS boards and firm career pages with two current 2027 trackers used for discovery. A role is counted only when its official application destination was live in that audit. The third-party trackers are discovery/cross-check sources, not substitutes for the linked employer page.

- **Coverage audit:** all 273 TraderMath firms remain in the company universe. The recheck attempted 30 official ATS boards (28 responded successfully), then cross-checked additional current official links. “No verified opening found” deliberately does not mean “confirmed closed.”

- **Dates:** exact start/end dates are included when the posting states them. Otherwise, the report gives the stated duration or says that exact dates were not published. “Deadline not stated” means applications were open but no closing date was shown.

- **Interview classification:** this is not a claim about every role or interview round. It reflects only the evidence currently visible on TraderMath and should be read role-by-role—especially for firms hiring both traders/researchers and software/hardware engineers.

- **Dynamic postings:** job boards change without notice. Re-open the application link before spending substantial time on an application.
